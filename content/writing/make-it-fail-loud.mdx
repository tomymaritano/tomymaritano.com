This site is drawn like a blueprint. Every page is a sheet, every visual is a figure, and every product gets a spec sheet with an honest status.

The mascot is **Blink**, a tiny terminal with a prompt for a face. Most of the time it idles. When something breaks, it does not hide it: the screen goes red and the antenna screams.

That is the whole idea. Silent failures are the expensive ones. Systems should crash early, log clearly and get fixed fast. Teams too.
